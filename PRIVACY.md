# Privacy Policy — HLG to SDR/HDR

Effective 23 September 2026

HLG to SDR/HDR for iPhone, iPad and Mac does not collect, store or transmit personal data.

- No account and no sign-in.
- No analytics, no advertising, no tracking, no third-party code.
- The app makes no network connections of its own. Every photo is read and converted on your device.
- On iPhone and iPad the app asks for access to your photo library for two things only: to read the original HLG files you choose, and to save the converted copies as new photos in the album you choose. It never modifies or deletes your originals.
- Files you pick in Files are copied into the app's temporary storage while they are in the queue, and removed when you clear the queue or the app starts again.
- If your originals are in iCloud Photos, iOS may download them from iCloud when you convert them. That transfer is made by Apple's Photos framework, under Apple's privacy policy.
- The app keeps its own settings, such as the interface language, on your device only.
